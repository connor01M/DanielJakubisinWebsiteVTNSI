Teaching

A teaching approach grounded in communication theory, signal processing, and the realities of resilient wireless operation.

Teaching Overview

The goal is to connect mathematical foundations, implementation tradeoffs, and modern wireless problem settings in a way that is useful to both students and practicing engineers.

The research portfolio suggests a teaching style that moves naturally between theory, signal-processing detail, machine learning, and operationally relevant communication challenges.

Topic Areas

  • Wireless communication foundations
  • Synchronization, detection, and receiver design
  • Resilient 5G and 6G systems
  • Distributed MIMO and coordination challenges
  • RF machine learning and interference suppression
  • Probabilistic signal processing

Mentoring Approach

  • Start with a clear conceptual model before turning to implementation.
  • Use recent publications as case studies for how ideas evolve into technical contributions.
  • Encourage students to connect theory, simulation, and clear technical writing early in a project.
