    Public Recognition

    • The IEEE Access survey on deep-learning-based interference suppression is currently the most cited item on the public Google Scholar profile.
    • Recent publications appear in IEEE Transactions on Communications, IEEE Access, MILCOM, ICC Workshops, VTC, OCEANS, and EuCNC/6G Summit.
    • Current public institutional profiles are available through the Virginia Tech National Security Institute and the Commonwealth Cyber Initiative.
    • DBLP and Scholar records show continued momentum in distributed MIMO, 6G communication, RF machine learning, and resilient wireless systems.

    Current Research Momentum

    • 2025: Papers on distributed uplink joint transmission, synchronization offsets, and sub-THz channel sounding broadened the current 6G and sensing-oriented portfolio.
    • 2024: Conference work continued across distributed-MIMO, agile 5G traffic steering, and RFML-driven interference mitigation.
    • 2022-2023: The public record shows continued visibility around deep-learning-based interference suppression and efficient RFML model design.
