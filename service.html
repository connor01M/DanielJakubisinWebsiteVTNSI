Service

Professional engagement, collaboration, and student-centered service connected to current research activities.

Research Community Engagement

  • Cross-unit work spanning the Virginia Tech National Security Institute, Bradley Electrical and Computer Engineering, and the Commonwealth Cyber Initiative.
  • Student mentorship around resilient wireless systems, RF machine learning, and learning-enabled communication systems.
  • Research collaboration that connects communication theory with practical wireless problems in contested and interference-heavy environments.
  • Translational conversations that bridge technical research, implementation, and broader impact.

Mentoring and Collaboration

Much of the visible service on this site is embedded in mentoring, project formation, collaborative writing, and helping students connect mathematical tools to research problems that matter in practice.

That includes guiding students into seminar-style research, shaping publication-oriented projects, and helping collaborators navigate the intersection of signal processing, wireless systems, and machine learning.

Related Professional Background

Prior work as a technical advisor supporting telecommunications intellectual property and patent matters adds a practical dimension to service-oriented activities that involve technical communication, system interpretation, and broader research translation.
